# Vision

## What this product is
Nexdo is an AI-native task manager built to help users move from planning to execution with more intelligence and momentum than traditional task tools.

## Who it serves
- founders and operators managing many moving priorities
- users who want more proactive task management, not just static lists
- people attracted to AI-native workflow support but frustrated by vague productivity software claims

## Core promise
Nexdo helps users prioritize, organize, and execute meaningful work with a more proactive and intelligent workflow layer.

## Current constraints
- repo/deploy/build verification still needs to be made explicit in the product operating layer
- product promise must stay grounded in what can actually be built and proven
- AI-native positioning needs concrete differentiated workflow behavior behind it

## Non-goals
- becoming a generic to-do list app with AI slapped on top
- relying on abstract productivity language without clear differentiated workflows
- treating branding as a substitute for verified execution capability

## What success looks like
- Nexdo has a verified repo/deploy path
- MVP scope is clear and execution-ready
- product promise is concrete enough to build and test
- Nexdo becomes an active, real product rather than a strong concept only
