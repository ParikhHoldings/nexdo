# Marketing

## ICP
- founders and operators with too many moving priorities
- users who feel traditional task managers are static and low-leverage
- people attracted to AI-native execution help but skeptical of empty hype
- AI power users who want a structured task layer their tools and agents can write to

## Positioning
Nexdo is an AI-native task manager built to move tasks from capture into prioritized, contextual, execution-ready work.

## Recommended First Revenue Wedge
Lead with founder/operator AI power users, not broad teams.

The first paid audience should be people who already feel task-list pain daily, already use AI tools, and can value a structured task layer before Nexdo has mature collaboration, admin, or enterprise features. This keeps the offer close to what the product currently supports: task capture, structured planning metadata, daily prioritization, reviewable bounded outputs, task handoffs, and scoped agent/API access.

Primary offer:
- "Turn messy work into a prioritized, agent-ready task layer."
- Free plan proves the demo and personal workflow.
- Pro is for higher task volume and daily execution use.
- Power is for AI power users who need API-key/MCP/ChatGPT Actions access.

Do not lead with:
- broad team project management
- enterprise security/compliance
- fully autonomous agents
- native OAuth integration depth
- open-ended task completion

Near-term proof target:
- 5 to 10 founder/operator design partners try the demo or early account flow.
- At least 3 capture real tasks, use a daily briefing or prioritization reason, and generate or copy one bounded output/handoff.
- At least 1 AI-power user verifies that scoped agent task access would be useful enough to justify the Power tier once real provider/API-key smokes pass.

## Main pain points
- task managers become graveyards of stale lists
- planning is disconnected from execution momentum
- users want help prioritizing and moving work, not just storing tasks
- AI productivity products often sound impressive but feel vague in practice
- agent workflows need a reliable task layer that humans can inspect and control

## Product-truth guardrails
- Safe to say as draft/internal truth: Nexdo supports structured task capture, task-detail notes and task links, copyable/restorable task handoffs, AI parsing, prioritization, daily briefing, bounded research/draft/prep execution for executable task types, imports, Settings task export, and API-key based agent interop including reviewable task-note appends in the codebase.
- Do not externally claim production reliability, broad autonomous execution, large user traction, enterprise security, or fully verified integrations until checked end to end.
- Public-facing copy must go through Quill before launch or outreach.
- Prefer "helps prioritize and prepare execution" over "does all your tasks" unless the specific task type and limitation are clear.
- The current safer public-copy direction is "bounded AI assistance for structured tasks," not open-ended autonomous work.
