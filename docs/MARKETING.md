# Marketing

## ICP
- founders and operators with too many moving priorities
- users who feel traditional task managers are static and low-leverage
- people attracted to AI-native execution help but skeptical of empty hype

## Positioning
Nexdo is an AI-native task manager built to help users move from planning into action with more intelligence and proactive structure.

## Main pain points
- task managers become graveyards of stale lists
- planning is disconnected from execution momentum
- users want help prioritizing and moving work, not just storing tasks
- AI productivity products often sound impressive but feel vague in practice

## Channel ideas
- founder/operator productivity positioning
- landing page and waitlist testing
- narrative around escaping passive task-list sprawl
- direct audience learning before broad positioning commitments

## Content angles
- your task manager should move work forward, not just hold tasks
- AI-native should mean execution leverage, not generic assistant fluff
- from list storage to real momentum

## Current experiments
- tighten MVP/product framing around the clearest differentiated workflow
- verify the build/deploy path before pushing broader marketing claims
